## Fixture source-data gap

The supplied test-data table gives profile-level net pay and a combined “selected fixed obligations” amount for A01–A03, but not the separate housing, utilities and debt-payment values or their source-line IDs. Those profile totals are preserved in the fixture manifest; individual field values must not be inferred from the combined subtotal. A04 specifies irregular client receipts but no verified net-pay figure, so it stays local and requires human review.
