What the assistant did not look at

Payments that could reveal health, religion, union membership, politics or a criminal record are left out. The assistant never saw what they were. why?

EU privacy law (GDPR Article 9, and Article 10 for criminal matters) does not allow this kind of information to be used for a loan assessment.

Your name, address, bank account number and employer are removed before anything is sent. The citizen service number is never read.

Age, postcode, nationality, gender and education are not used.
